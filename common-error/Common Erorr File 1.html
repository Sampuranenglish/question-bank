Q1. The following sentence has been divided into four parts. Identify the part which contains a grammatical error.
The company will invest more(A) six hundred crores(B) in the next five years to expand(C) its operations in Britain (D).
Error Spotting · SSC CGL Tier 2 | 2025 | 18 Jan 2026
  • (a)A
  • (b)C
  • (c)B
  • (d)D
Answer: (a) A

Explanation:
⚠️ Note: the official key marks option (a) (part A), and that is the intended error; strictly, part B also has a slip — after a number crore stays singular (six hundred crore), so the sentence carries a second, minor error.
Rule: more comparing an amount with a figure must be followed by than (से अधिक).
Example: She scored more than 90 marks.
Without than, more six hundred crores says nothing.
Correct it.
The company will invest more than six hundred crore in the next five years to expand its operations in Britain.
Answer: A
Why the others are fine.
• C — in the next five years to expand — in the next five years is the right time phrase; to expand is an infinitive of purpose (उद्देेश्य बताने वाला infinitive).
• D — its operations in Britain — its (no apostrophe) is the possessive that matches the singular company (एकवचन सर्वनाम).
The second slip in B.
After a definite number, hundred, thousand, lakh, crore, million, dozen stay singular: six hundred crore, five lakh, two dozen (संख्या के बाद crore एकवचन).
The plural is used only without a number: crores of rupees. More … than: a comparison is not complete without “than”.
