Q1. Given below are four jumbled sentences. Para Jumbles · SSC CHSL Tier 2 | 2025 | 10 Apr 2026
  • (a)QSPR
  • (b)SPRQ
  • (c)PRSQ
  • (d)RSPQ
Answer: (d) RSPQ

Explanation:
Opener test. R (“Typically, a balanced skincare routine begins with…”) sets up the topic and the first step; S (Additionally), P (In contrast) and Q (Hence) each need an earlier sentence. Only (d) begins with R.
Links. R → S: Additionally gives the next step – actives go on clean, damp skin, just washed by R’s cleanser. S → P: In contrast shows the wrong way – a heavy moisturizer first blocks lighter serums. P → Q: Hence concludes the rule – thinnest to thickest.
Word help. efficacy = power to produce the intended result; absorption = soaking in.

Answer: (a) PRQS

Explanation:
⚠ Note. The official key marks (c) RQPS, but PRQS reads better.
