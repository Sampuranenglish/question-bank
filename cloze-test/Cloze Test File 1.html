Q1. What should come in the place of blank number 1?

  • (b)Balances
  • (c)Gaps
  • (d)Faults
Answer: (c) Gaps

Explanation:
The deciding words. "politicians take advantage of (1) [blank] within the system. Gradual removal of checks and balances …" What the blank needs. A plural noun for something politicians can exploit inside the system — a weakness or opening. gaps = openings, loopholes — exactly what one "takes advantage of". Answer: (c) Gaps
Why the others are wrong
• strength = power, firmness — the paragraph is about how institutions weaken; exploiting strength makes no sense, and it is singular.
• balances = counterweights — the next line speaks of removing checks and balances, so they are what is lost, not what is exploited.
• faults = defects, mistakes — close, but faults suggests blame or errors; the passage means loopholes, openings that can be used. Word help. resilient = able to recover quickly from difficulty . checks and balances = the limits each branch of government places on the others . You take advantage of gaps (loopholes) in a system, not of its faults.
