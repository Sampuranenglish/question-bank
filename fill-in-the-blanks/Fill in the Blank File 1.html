If the weather forecast proves accurate, we ______ heavy snowfall in the region tomorrow.

  • (b)are having
  • (c)must have
  • (d)should have
Answer: (d) should have

Explanation:
The correct sentence is:
If the weather forecast proves accurate, we should have heavy snowfall in the region tomorrow.
"Should have" is used to indicate a likely or expected future outcome based on a condition (i.e., if the forecast is correct).
"Need to have" (Option (a) implies necessity, which doesn’t fit the context of a weather prediction.
"Are having" (Option (b) is present continuous and does not match the conditional future context.
"Must have" (Option (c) implies certainty or obligation, which is not appropriate for a weather forecast. Therefore, Option (d): should have is the most appropriate choice.

Explanation:
The correct verb form after "decided" is the infinitive form (to + base verb).

Now examine the options:

3. undertake – incorrect (bare verb form; not used directly after "decided")

"She decided to undertake the challenging task of learning a new language during her summer break."
